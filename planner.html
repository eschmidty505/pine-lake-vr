Walk the yard

  • WASD / arrows or the joystick to walk · Shift to jog · tap the map to walk there
  • E or Inspect: open the nearest plant, or the zone you're standing in
  • Tap a plant to select it, then drag it to move it. Setback checks run while you drag.
  • Pick a plant in the hotbar or Catalog, then tap the map (or Space / Plant here) to plant it
  • Del removes the selected plant · Ctrl+Z undo · wheel or pinch to zoom · C re-centers
  • The year slider grows everything; the month row shows bloom, fruit, fall color and winter.
