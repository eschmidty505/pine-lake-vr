      Approvals & permits

      WhoNeeded?Why
      Pine Crest Developer (Lombardo), later the ACCYesBylaws Art. VI 3(f): no deck without written approval, and decks are rear yard only. This spot is rear yard. Submit the plan and elevation from this page.
      Utility easementsClearVI 3(ff) bans decks over utility easements. The nearest is the strip along the side lot lines, far from the house.
      Almont Twp building permitYesMichigan Residential Code: decks more than 30″ above grade need a permit. This one is 9′-8″ up. Expect footing-hole and final inspections. Ask whether they want an engineer's stamp for a freestanding deck this tall.
      Lapeer County Health (septic, well)NoThe septic field is in the front yard. The well is 18′ west of the deck's west edge, past the 10′ setback.
      Electrical permitOnly ifAn outlet or light added after closing needs its own permit. Done by Lombardo before drywall, it rides on the house permit. The grill runs on propane tanks, so no gas work.
      MISS DIG 811YesCall before digging footings. The well line, sump lead and any electric run sit behind the house.
