Install phases

The whole yard split into planting seasons you can afford, ordered by what you care about most: getting fruit and nuts sooner, food that carries you through winter, getting slow trees in the ground early, or the patio and play areas. Drag the weights, set a budget per season, and pin anything to a different phase.

What goes first

Fruit + nuts ranks each crop by its first ten years of harvest per dollar, so fast, cheap producers (raspberries, haskap, the veggie beds) jump ahead. Sustaining food ranks by calories in the first ten years per dollar, so potatoes, dry beans, hazels and chestnuts rise. Trees + privacy ranks by how long a tree takes to reach ¾ of its full size, so slow giants like the bur oak and white pines go in early.
