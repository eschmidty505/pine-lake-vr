Install phases

The whole yard split into planting seasons you can afford, ordered by what you care about most: getting fruit and nuts sooner, getting slow trees in the ground early, or the patio and play areas. Drag the weights, set a budget per season, and pin anything to a different phase.

What goes first

Fruit + nuts ranks each crop by its first ten years of harvest per dollar, so fast, cheap producers (raspberries, haskap, the veggie beds) jump ahead. Trees + privacy ranks by how long a tree takes to reach ¾ of its full size, so slow giants like the bur oak and white pines go in early.
