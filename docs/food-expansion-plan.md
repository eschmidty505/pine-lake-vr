## 0. Why we're doing this

Erik's current saved plan (plan **E**, "legit manual save") produces about **806 lb of food a year** at maturity:

| Source | lb/yr |
|---|---|
| Fruit | ~650 |
| Vegetables | ~106 |
| Nuts | ~52 |

That's about $3,000/yr at grocery prices.

For a family of 4, it covers:

- about **7% of calories**
- about 80% of fruit on paper (≈50% realistically)
- about 10% of vegetables

The plan is fruit-heavy. Fruit is mostly water and sugar, so it doesn't carry a household through winter.

**Goal:** raise the share of *sustaining* food (calories, protein, foods that store) to about **20% of calories** without blowing the budget. The levers:

1. **Calorie crops in beds:** potatoes, winter squash, dry beans, garlic/onions.
2. **Nut trees and shrubs** that actually bear: hybrid hazelnut and Chinese chestnut.
3. **Low-input native fruit trees:** American persimmon and pawpaw.
4. **Perennial vegetables** that come back every year: asparagus, rhubarb, walking onions, horseradish.
5. **A dedicated strawberry bed.**
6. **Season extension:** low tunnels.
7. **Storage:** root cellar corner, chest freezer.
8. **Ornamental→edible swaps** in spots where the plan already has a tree or shrub.
9. **Chickens: TBD** (see §6).

The rough target is about **+360,000 kcal/yr** at maturity, which takes calories from about 7% to about 20%.

### Coordinates and data model (planner.html)

- **Lot grid:** in feet. x runs east, y runs north. In 3D, a point maps to `(x, elev − 870, −y)`.
- **Entity kinds:** `plant`, `row`, `bed` (polygon + material), `path`, `st` (structure).
- **Species registry:** `SP`. Add species with these helpers:

```js
S(k, { n, v, l, c, nat, ed, ev, h0, hM, s0, sM, tau, bl, fr, leaf, bc, fc, fall, buy, note, roots, warn, row, sp, reach, form })
PER(k, name, latin, bloomMonths, bloomColor, extra)   // c:'perennial'
GR(k, name, latin, extra)                              // c:'grass'
```

What the species fields mean:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `c` | Category: `conifer` \| `tree` \| `fruit` \| `berry` \| `shrub` \| `perennial` \| `grass` |
| `h0`, `hM` | Height at planting / at maturity (ft) |
| `s0`, `sM` | Spread at planting / at maturity (ft) |
| `tau` | Growth time-constant in years. Size follows `grow = a + (b−a)(1 − e^(−yrs/tau))`. |
| `bl`, `fr` | Bloom and fruit months (1–12). These drive the season colors and the card calendars. |
| `leaf`, `bc`, `fc`, `fall` | Hex colors for leaves, bloom, fruit and fall foliage |
| `ed`, `nat`, `ev` | Flags: edible, native, evergreen |
| `warn` | Shown on the card and checked in `checks()` |
| `row: 1`, `sp` | Row plant (canes) with spacing `sp` |
| `reach` | Root setback in ft |
| `form` | Silhouette for the cards: `'column'` or `'oval'`. Otherwise `formOf()` infers it. |

Other things to know:

- **Pricing:** `PLANT_PRICE` by category (conifer 165, tree 185, fruit 45, berry 18, shrub 35, perennial 9, grass 9) and `ROW_PRICE` 6/ft.
  - To price a species differently from its category, add a per-species `price` field and make the cost code prefer it. Today everything is priced by category.
- **Structures:** `SD`, grouped by `SD_GROUPS`. Tall ones go in `TALL`.
  - 2D drawing is in `drawStLocal()`'s `switch(e.k)`; 3D is in `st3D(e)`.
  - Kitchen-garden beds come from `kgBeds()`.
- **Bed materials:** `BM` (`paved`/`hard` flags), grouped by `BM_GROUPS`.
- **Rules:** `checks(e, all)` returns `block` / `err` / `warn` messages.
- **3D models:**
  - `plantGeo(s, h, w, m, v, lite)` builds plant meshes, cached in `PCACHE`.
  - `formOf(s)` and `drawPlantIcon()` draw the card silhouettes.
  - `HOTPLANTS` is the "popular" shortlist.

### Phases data model (phase_data.py → phases.html)

Each package has these fields:

```
id, name, group, cost, deps[], season (fall|spring|any), must,
H/T/L axes, crop{first, full, lb, price, unit, qty, months, color, src},
mature{h, w, t75}, nonEss, short
```

- `CROPS[k] = dict(first, full, lb, price, unit ('plant'|'ft'|'sf'), src)`
- `CROPNAME[k]` is the display name.
- **Scoring:**
  - Harvest score = first-10-year value per dollar.
  - Tree score = `t75 × height`.
  - Balance = a slider between the two.
- **Scheduler:** greedy, with a per-season spending cap. Pins are stored in localStorage under `plp-state`.
- **Non-essential items are excluded from the budget:** the hoop house ($1,800) and the pergola ($3,200). Keep it that way.

### 3.10 Calorie crops (bed-based, rotated yearly)

These are the core of the calorie gain. Model them as **one new bed type, "Calorie bed"**, with a crop selector. Don't make them individual plants.

- **Crop selector:** potatoes / winter squash / dry beans / garlic+onions / sweet potato (optional) / sunchoke (optional, see the warning below).
- **Each crop's numbers are per sf** (table below). The bed's area sets the yield.
- **Rotation (stretch):** a year selector that cycles crops across beds (potatoes → beans → squash → garlic), so the 3D/2D labels change with `ST.year`.

| key | Crop | lb/sf/yr | kcal/lb | $/lb | First | Months | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `potato` | Potatoes | 0.75 | 350 | 1.2 | 1 | 7–9 | Hill in loose raised beds. Clay will rot them. Stores 4–6 mo at 40°F. |
| `wsquash` | Winter squash (butternut, 'Waltham') | 0.6 | 200 | 1.8 | 1 | 9–10 | Sprawls about 25 sf/plant. Can trail off the bed onto mulch. Stores 3–6 mo. |
| `drybean` | Dry beans ('Jacob's Cattle', 'Black Turtle') | 0.1 | 1550 | 3 | 1 | 9 | Low lb but highest calories and protein. No pressure-canning needed. |
| `garlic` | Garlic (hardneck 'Music') | 0.2 | 680 | 10 | 1 (plant Oct, harvest Jul) | 7 | Fall-planted, so it frees the bed for a summer crop of beans. |
| `onion` | Storage onions ('Copra') | 0.5 | 180 | 1.5 | 1 | 8 | Long-day types for Michigan. |
| `sweetpot` | Sweet potato (optional) | 0.6 | 390 | 2 | 1 | 9–10 | Needs black plastic plus a low tunnel in zone 6. |
| `sunchoke` | Sunchoke (optional) | 1.0 | 330 | 3 | 1 | 10–11 | **Invasive spreader.** Only in a sunk container or an isolated strip. Default off. |

- **Area target:** **400–600 sf** of calorie beds. For example, 4–6 beds of 4×24 (a kit like `raised` but longer), or one in-ground amended block of about 20×25.
- **Placement:** **east of the house**, outside the septic field, reserve, easements and the well's 50′ radius (manure and compost go here). Full sun.
  - Next to the existing vegetable beds (`bed-Z10`) so it can share the drip and the potting bench.
- **Implementation suggestion:** `SD.calbed = { n: 'Calorie bed 4×24', g: 'Garden', w: 4, h: 24, cost: 260, crop: 'potato' }` plus a property-panel dropdown for `e.crop`.
  - **2D:** soil color with crop-specific texture (potato hills / squash leaves / bean rows).
  - **3D:** draw from `st3D` the same way as `raised`, with low mounded foliage colored by crop.
- **`checks()`:** make sure calorie beds block on the septic field and reserve the same way current edible beds do.

Rough calorie math at 500 sf. This is the bulk of the +360k target:

- 200 sf potatoes × 0.75 × 350 ≈ 52k kcal
- 150 sf squash × 0.6 × 200 ≈ 18k
- 100 sf beans × 0.1 × 1550 ≈ 15.5k
- 50 sf garlic ≈ 7k

That's about 93k kcal/yr from the beds alone. Nuts (≈ 150k at maturity: 2 chestnuts × 50 lb × 900, plus hazels), persimmon, pawpaw and the existing apples make up the rest.
