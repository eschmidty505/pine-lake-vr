      Approvals & permits

      WhoNeeded?Why
      Pine Crest Developer (Lombardo), later the ACCYesBylaws Art. VI 3(f): no deck without written approval, and decks are rear yard only. This spot is rear yard. Submit the plan and elevation from this page.
      Utility easementsClearVI 3(ff) bans decks over utility easements. The nearest is the 12′ strip on the side lines, far from the house.
      Almont Twp building permitYesMichigan Residential Code: decks more than 30″ above grade or attached to the house need a permit. This one is 9′-8″ up. Expect footing-hole and final inspections. Ask whether they want a drawing stamped by an engineer for a freestanding deck this tall.
      Lapeer County Health (septic, well)NoThe septic field is in the front yard. The well is 18′+ from the deck's west edge, past the 10′ setback.
      Electrical / gas permitsOnly ifAn outlet, light or gas stub added after closing needs its own permit. Done by Lombardo before drywall, it rides on the house permit.
      MISS DIG 811YesCall before digging footings. The well line, sump lead and any rear-yard electric run behind the house.
